Two things this model does not count

HUHC holders are excluded from two further flexible-pool streams. The FY2026/27 schedule labels them “Services to Improve Access (Non-HUHC)” and “Health Promotion (Non-HUHC)”. A HUHC patient generates $0.00 in both. On observed data that is roughly $16 per HUHC patient per year on top of everything above.

Care Plus is PHO funding, not practice funding. Health NZ pays the pool to the PHO, which distributes it. A practice figure here is an attributable share of the deduction — what lands in your settlement depends on your PHO's distribution rules.

How this is calculated

The deduction

Health NZ's Care Plus formula sums a per-person Care Plus percentage across the enrolled population, then subtracts the number of Enrolled Persons with High Use Health Cards. It is a whole-person subtraction, so the marginal cost of HUHC status is exactly one Care Plus payment.

Care Plus rates

$296.8718 per expected Care Plus patient from 1 July 2026, per the published FY2026/27 capitation workbook. The prior rate of $289.18 is editable above if your own figure differs.

The top-up rate is your input

The FY2026/27 schedule carries five capitation streams — First Contact, Contingent, VLCA, CSC and U14 — and no HUHC column, because the rates were removed. Prior-year schedules are not publicly retrievable, so the withdrawn rate has to be supplied from your own funding records. Everything else here is published.

Care Plus potential

The expected Care Plus patient count, not a headcount — it is a sum of per-person probabilities. Across one PHO's 69 practices it ran between 3.5% and 10.3% of the roll; 6.9% is used as the estimate default. Enter your own if you have it.

Scope

Annual figures, GST exclusive. First-level capitation for the patient is unaffected by HUHC status and is not modelled, because it does not change with HUHC status either side of 1 July.
